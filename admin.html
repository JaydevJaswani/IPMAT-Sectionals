Item calibration what the students' data says about each question

Difficulty is measured, not declared: Observed is what the cohort's accuracy and solving time actually imply, on the same 4–8 scale the paper was authored on. Drift is how far the authored guess was out. Disc is discrimination — whether the question separates strong candidates from weak ones. A question near zero discrimination is dead weight however hard it looks; a negative one usually means the key is wrong.
